# Open facts and launch decisions

1. **Collateral custody:** confirm who holds the time-locked collateral, title, control rights, release conditions, liquidation mechanics, LTV, and jurisdiction. The site makes no custodial or non-custodial claim.
2. **Investor access:** Sites reported a public audience when the visual redesign began; that setting is preserved. Implement a server-side qualified/accredited investor review before distributing private offering material. The existing request form is not authentication and no private document delivery is implemented. Confirm permitted public marketing language per market.
3. **Inquiry delivery:** configure `VITE_CONTACT_ENDPOINT` only after deploying a secure receiving service with server-side Zod-equivalent validation, rate limits, retention policy, and consent records. Currently submissions are disabled, so no inquiry is silently lost.
4. **Privacy and legal:** insert the legal entity/controller, physical or electronic privacy contact, lawful basis, retention period, cookie/hosting disclosures, applicable securities and lending disclosures, and local counsel review for Guatemala, Costa Rica, Mexico and the United States.
5. **Targets and structure:** confirm 10–15% annual yield target, 4–5% flat preferred return per tour leg, 30–90-day duration, priority liens, waterfall fee base, and whether fees may be charged together. No source document proves realized return or perfected security.
6. **Market estimates:** obtain external citations and measurement definitions for the deck's USD 40B TAM and USD 15B+ working-capital need before public launch. They are labeled company estimates here.
7. **Team biographies and roles:** the source deck is used as supplied, correcting Edwin's surname to Kestler; verify all titles and claims with the named people before public launch.
8. **Forecast integration:** replace synthetic events only through an authenticated, server-side CT-GOT adapter with permissions and freshness metadata. Never ship engine credentials to the browser. Example probability thresholds are invented solely for the clearly labeled demonstration.
9. **Pilot:** dates, investors, promoters, venues, and transaction documents remain to be established. The 90-day sequence is a proposal, not achieved milestones.
10. **Public launch:** review `robots.txt`, sitemap origin, canonical links, and test with Lighthouse and accessibility tooling on the final public host. Existing crawler exclusion is unchanged; it is not an access control. The redesign does not certify Lighthouse scores or full WCAG conformance.
11. **Waterfall economics:** confirm whether F&B is in distributable revenue for each event, whether the platform fee is assessed on gross or net receipts, the promoter/artist split, tax/withholding, refund seniority, shortfall recovery, and settlement currency. The calculator uses a documented illustrative net-revenue fee base and isolates operating deficits.
12. **Editorial imagery:** the concert hero is AI-generated conceptual imagery, disclosed in both languages. It does not represent an actual CORTEX show, artist, venue, client, or partner. Replace only with an appropriately licensed or owned event photograph if real-event imagery becomes available.
